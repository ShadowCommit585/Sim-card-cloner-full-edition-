What is a SIM Card Cloner?

A SIM card cloner is a hardware and software tool used to duplicate the sensitive identifying data of an original Subscriber Identity Module (SIM) onto a blank programmable SIM card.

This process can cause multiple SIM cards to share the same mobile network identity and phone number.

How SIM Card Cloning Works

1. Hardware Components

  • USB Smart-Card Reader/Writer
  • SIM Analysis Software
  • Programmable Blank SIM Card
  • Telecom Diagnostic Tools

2. Data Extraction

SIM cloning focuses on extracting two important identifiers:

  • IMSI — International Mobile Subscriber Identity
  • Ki — Authentication Key

3. SIM Programming

After extraction, the IMSI and Ki values are written onto a blank programmable SIM card.

Security Risks

  • SMS 2FA Interception
  • Financial Account Hijacking
  • Unauthorized Calls & Messages
  • Identity Theft
  • Mobile Banking Fraud
  • Email & Social Media Takeovers
